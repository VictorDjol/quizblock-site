Block distracting sites. Learn your way back in.

QuizBlock is a free site blocker for Chrome. Open YouTube, Instagram or Reddit and you get a quick challenge instead of the feed: find ten countries on a map, or get a few questions right. Finish it and the site opens for a while.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store Free. No account. Nothing leaves your browser.

Learn something every time

Every unlock teaches you something real.

  • Interactive maps of every continent, with all 193 UN member states.
  • Over 1,500 hand-checked questions across geography, history, science, nature, art, music, sport and more.
  • Spaced repetition: anything you miss comes back until it sticks.

Built to actually work

Blockers fail when they're easy to talk your way around. QuizBlock isn't.

  • The challenge is chosen for you, and reloading won't re-roll it.
  • Time only counts while the site is open, and unused time lapses at midnight.
  • Loosening a setting waits until tomorrow. Tightening is instant.
  • Focus hours, uncancellable focus locks, and blocking just part of a site, like YouTube Shorts.

See your week

A weekly recap shows what you learned, how many challenges you took and how often you walked away instead, compared with the week before.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your sites, settings and progress stay in your browser. Read the privacy policy.
